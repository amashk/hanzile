Levels

There are three puzzles every day, one per level. Pick the one that matches the characters you know:
Apprentice — HSK 1–2 · Scholar — HSK 3–5 · Master — HSK 6–9

You can guess any character. A character shown gray and dashed while you type or draw isn't on your level's list, so it can't be the answer — but its overlap still counts as a hint.

About Hanzile

Hanzile is a free daily Chinese character guessing game, a bit like Wordle for hanzi (汉字). Every day there's a hidden Chinese character, and you have six guesses to find it. Each guess reveals the strokes your character shares with the hidden one, and scores how close the shapes are.

Three levels, three puzzles a day

Pick the level that matches the characters you know. Each one has its own daily puzzle, drawn from the HSK 3.0 character lists:

  • Apprentice: HSK 1–2, about 600 of the most basic characters
  • Scholar: HSK 3–5, about 900 intermediate characters
  • Master: HSK 6–9, 1,500 advanced characters

You can guess any character, even one from outside your level, to get a better hint. When the game ends you see the answer's pinyin and meaning, so every round teaches you a character.

Hard mode

In hard mode each guess only reveals one quarter of the board, and the score only compares that quarter.

Type or draw

Type characters with your pinyin keyboard, or draw them by hand on the board and pick from the recognized characters. Drawing is handy for characters you can write but can't pronounce.

Who it's for

Hanzile is made for Mandarin learners practicing HSK characters, and for anyone who reads Chinese and enjoys a quick daily puzzle. A new set of puzzles starts every day at midnight, your local time.

Hanzile is made by the creator of Atone, the app for hearing and practicing Mandarin tones. Hanzile teaches you the shape of a character; Atone helps you say it right.
